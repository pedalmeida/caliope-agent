## Brand rules (draft, from Pedro 2026-10-17)

1. Calm and premium beats "out of the box" when they collide. Calm means disciplined, not soft.
2. Zero AI slop: no generic gradients, no stock, no glow, no emoji decoration, no mockups that show nothing real.
3. Neutrals plus one accent (the brand orange). A second accent needs a written reason.
4. One display voice and one body voice. A third family needs a written reason.
5. One claim per section, one CTA per screen.
6. Flat, small-radius shapes. Hard offset shadows and thick borders only where they earn their place.
7. Evidence beats decoration: real screenshots, terminals, numbers.
8. Consistency beats cleverness: repeat the pattern.
9. The orange law: the brand orange `#F26B1F` never carries text on cream (2.87:1); text orange is `#C93A12`.
