## Update after Pedro's answer (2026-10-17)

Pedro likes all six and ranks robertocortez.pt first: "simple, not AI slop, consistent, premium and distinctive feeling".

**Correction to my earlier reading:** I wrote that only joaomoita.com matched "calm premium". Pedro's top pick is the dark, heavy-condensed, red one. So for him "premium" is not cream and serif. It is restraint and consistency: few moving parts, strictly applied. Calm means disciplined, not soft.

Measured on robertocortez.pt (home, DOM computed styles, not a design-system export):
- 2 families only: Barlow Condensed (headlines, weight 800, uppercase, letter-spacing about -2px at 57px) and Barlow (body).
- Colour: white and near-black (#111) surfaces, one red (#D71629), greys for body text. Nothing else.
- Buttons with 3px radius. No soft pills, no big rounded cards.
- 9 sections, 7500px page, a single claim per section.

Working hypothesis, to confirm with Pedro: the premium feel comes from a tiny palette (neutrals plus one accent), one typographic voice, hard-edged shapes and strict repetition. That is compatible with the v2 DNA (Archivo heavy, one orange) but it asks for far fewer elements: no yellow, no pink, no mascot-everywhere, no Silkscreen, softer shadows or none.

Rules derived (draft):
1. Neutrals plus ONE accent. Nothing else enters.
2. One display voice, one body voice. No third family.
3. One claim per section, one CTA per screen.
4. Shapes: small radius, flat. Hard shadows only if they earn it.
5. Consistency over cleverness: same pattern repeated beats a new trick per slide.

## Carousel style board (added 2026-11-11, from Pedro)

Screenshots: `img/2026-11-11-carousel-refs-1.webp`, `img/2026-11-11-carousel-refs-2.webp` (a creator's AI-news and advisory carousels, the We Call Shotgun / Spicy Advisory family). Pedro: "references for the style, gradients, colour palette and illustration style". Also: the trishul mark "looks awkward", redo it or find another idea; the rest is better but needs more personality.

What to take:
- Two surfaces that alternate: deep maroon-to-black with a hot orange radial glow (covers, big claims), and warm cream (content). Never a flat dark.
- Condensed heavy display, very large, two lines, one orange word per headline.
- The creator as a painted caricature mascot on almost every slide, full body, pointing at or reacting to the content. Hoodie, sneakers, same outfit every time: the mascot is the consistency.
- Small orange line icons with a soft glow on dark; numbered orange circles for steps; one stat hero (74%) with a waffle grid.
- Mono kicker top left (01 / 08), source line bottom.

What not to copy: their character, their outfit (the cream hoodie), their chili mark, their name styling. Pedro's mascot wears his own colours.
