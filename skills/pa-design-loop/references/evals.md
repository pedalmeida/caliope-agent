# Step 4: answers to evals

## From a pick to a check

Every pick and every never-list item becomes one or more binary checks a reviewer can score from a screenshot or the source, with evidence. Write them in Pedro's words where possible.

| Answer | Check |
|---|---|
| 1A light, white cards on grey | No dark banners, hero blocks or panels on any screen. Cards are white on a light grey canvas. |
| 2A 4 KPIs, lots of air | The overview shows at most 4 KPI tiles and at most 4 main sections; no section shows more than 6 items before "View all". At least 24 px between sections. |
| never: emoji | No emoji anywhere in the UI. |
| Job "what needs me right now?" | The answer is visible above the fold on desktop and within the first screen (812 px) on phone. |

A check that cannot be scored PASS or FAIL from evidence is rewritten until it can.

## Compose `evals.md`

1. **A. Pedro's checks**: from the interview and the brief's 3 jobs. This is the part he signs off.
2. **B. Brand and Calíope principles** and **C. Craft floor**: copied from `standing-rubric.md`, minus any check the project's brand profile overrides (say which and why).
3. **D. Pedro's learned checks**: the dated list at the end of `standing-rubric.md`, all of them, every time.

Number every check (A1, B3...) so scorecards across rounds compare line by line.

## Sign-off

Show Pedro section A only, as a short list. He replies "ok" or edits. His edit wins and is recorded verbatim.

## Scoring

Every reviewer returns a table: check id, PASS/FAIL, evidence (which screenshot, where). A check with no evidence counts as FAIL. The score is passes over total, reported per round.
